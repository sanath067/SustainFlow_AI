MODEL SELECTION RATIONALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
LightGBM is preferred over Linear Regression because it achieves
a much higher R2 (0.9367 vs 0.7364) on the test set.

Why: Demand is driven by non-linear effects such as lag history
(lag_1, lag_2, lag_3), rolling averages and seasonal components.
Linear Regression can only fit straight-line relationships between
these features and demand, missing spikes and seasonal patterns.

LightGBM is a gradient-boosted tree model that naturally learns
non-linear feature interactions (e.g. lag_1 x month) and handles
the many encoded categories efficiently, making it a better fit
for time-series demand forecasting.
